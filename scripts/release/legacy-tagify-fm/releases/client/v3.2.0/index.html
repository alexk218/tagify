What's new in Tagify 3.2.0

New Stats, quicker tagging and more reliable Smart Playlists.

See the music behind your stats
Explore your tagging calendar, streaks, favorite tags, ratings, artists, tempo, energy and musical keys. Open a chart’s songs to search, play or turn them into a playlist.

You can also choose to share your tagging calendar, longest streak, average rating and favorite tag pairs on your Tagify Community profile!

Your tags follow matching releases
Spotify often has multiple releases of the same recording, like a single and an album release. Tagify detects matching copies and keeps your tags, ratings and energy consistent across them.

+ other minor bug fixes!

Thanks to everyone who tested and reported problems!

Install or update

- macOS: Download Tagify Manager, move it to Applications and open it to install or update Tagify.
- Windows: Download the Windows installer and run it to install or update Tagify.
